summary: "Three ways to estimate a target pace, from cheapest to most accurate."

Before you can hold a pace you need to pick one. Three methods, in increasing order
of cost and accuracy:

1. **Recent race equivalence.** Take a hard effort from the last six weeks and
   convert it. A 20-minute 5K maps to roughly a 1:32 half marathon for a
   well-trained runner — less if your endurance base is thin.
2. **Threshold testing.** Run 30 minutes as hard as you can hold evenly. Your
   average pace over the final 20 minutes is a workable approximation of lactate
   threshold. Most marathon paces sit 8–12% slower than that.
3. **Lab testing.** Precise, expensive, and mostly useful if you are already doing
   everything else right.
