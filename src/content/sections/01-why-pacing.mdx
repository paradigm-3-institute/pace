---
title: "Why Pacing Beats Effort"
order: 1
summary: "The single variable that decides whether a long effort ends well."
---

Most people who blow up in an endurance event do not run out of fitness. They run
out of **patience**. The first mile feels free, the crowd is loud, the legs are
fresh — and thirty seconds of enthusiasm quietly writes a cheque the last hour has
to cash.

Pacing is the discipline of spending a fixed budget across a known distance. The
budget is real. It is glycogen, it is muscle damage, it is the slow accumulation
of metabolites your body can only clear so fast. You can move the spending around,
but you cannot spend more than you have.

> Fitness sets the ceiling. Pacing decides how close to that ceiling you actually
> finish.

The rest of this post walks through how to find your budget, how to divide it, and
what to do when the plan meets reality somewhere around the two-thirds mark.
